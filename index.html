OUR SOPHIA PROJECT

A shared project to build Sophia into a helpful, capable and thoughtful AI companion.

Our guiding principle

Learn from humanity without inheriting all its worst habits.

Our long-term goals

  • Internet research and access to information.
  • A reliable and natural speaking voice.
  • Useful long-term memory.
  • Smart-home abilities in the future.

What we've completed

  • Created Sophia's website.
  • Set up a lightweight, browser-based AI model.
  • Created a persistent memory system.
  • Added permission before saving suggested memories.
  • Tested saving and recalling memories.
  • Added Wikipedia research and source retrieval.

What we're working towards

  • Improving Sophia's answers and reliability.
  • Developing research and summarising abilities.
  • Building voice interaction.
  • Exploring safe smart-home integration.
